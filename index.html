Before you start

How it works

  • Answer one question at a time. You can only answer each question once.
  • You see straight away why an answer is right and why a wrong choice is wrong.
  • Drag the words into the boxes. On a phone or tablet you can also tap a word, then tap a box.
  • Every question is worth 1 mark. In a drag question you get part of the mark for each correct box.
  • After you answer, the clue words are highlighted. Learn to spot them in the next question.
